# Third-party notices

## WorkSphere job-source adapters

Parts of the Job Radar source-discovery logic were adapted from the open-source
WorkSphere project by Pawwo, particularly its Just Join IT, RocketJobs,
No Fluff Jobs, TheProtocol and Pracuj.pl search adapters.

Source: https://github.com/Pawwo/WorkSphere
License: Apache License 2.0

RoleTailor's implementation is a substantial rewrite in Rust for the local Tauri
runtime. Modified files carry RoleTailor-specific models, filtering, lifecycle
checks, scoring and persistence.
